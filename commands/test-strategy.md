Design a test strategy for: $ARGUMENTS

Use the `test-strategist`, `test-architect`, `qa-fundamentals`, `microservices-testing` and `cicd-testing` skills.

1. Understand the architecture and stack from the repository; note risky areas and current test state.
2. Choose the right shape (pyramid, trophy or honeycomb) and the tests per level, with tool recommendations that fit the stack.
3. Define automation scope, environments and data, CI stages and quality gates, and the metrics to track (`test-metrics-reporting`).
4. Give a phased 30/60/90-day rollout with the first three actions that pay off fastest.
