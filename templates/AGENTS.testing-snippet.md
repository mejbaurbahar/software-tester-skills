## Testing (software-tester-skills)

For any testing, QA, test-planning, test-review or bug-report task:
1. Consult the `qa-skill-router` skill and load the 1-3 skills it recommends.
2. Follow the loaded skill's procedure; do not improvise a weaker one.
3. Run security, fuzz and load tests only against systems we own or are authorized to test, preferably staging, with no real customer data or live payment credentials.
4. Report bugs in the `bug-reporting` format; finish with what was tested, what was not, and residual risk.

Install: `curl -fsSL https://mejbaurbahar.github.io/software-tester-skills/install.sh | bash -s -- --project`
